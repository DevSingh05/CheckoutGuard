# HackPSU challenge fit

The supplied **Hacker's Guide Fall '26** describes FNB's challenge as helping people make smarter financial decisions at important moments, moving from information to understanding, confidence, and action. It describes Nittany AI as building something that improves everyday life through AI.

CheckoutGuard targets the moment before an unfamiliar-store purchase:

| Track | Concrete product behavior |
| --- | --- |
| FNB — Trust, Decisions & Financial Confidence | Turns linked buyer reports into understandable evidence, separates unavailable research from lower risk, and gives practical next steps before money changes hands. |
| Nittany AI | Uses Backboard's AI-assisted public research and GPTZero's uncertain text signal for an everyday shopper problem, while keeping scoring deterministic and limits visible. |
