# CheckoutGuard — two-minute judge walkthrough

## Before the timer

Use the current v0.2.0 backend and extension. Keep the API running. Reload the extension and product tab after a build. Open Service connection & setup to confirm the server version and configured-source flags. Keep credentials and account pages out of view.

Use a public storefront for the extraction demonstration. If successful live-source verification has not passed, say that directly and switch explicitly to the fictional scenarios for the result walkthrough. Do not pretend a fixture scan is live research.

## 0:00–0:20 — The purchase decision

“An unfamiliar store can look polished. CheckoutGuard gives shoppers a pause before money changes hands.”

Open the side panel next to the product page. Point to the detected domain and title. Explain that only an explicit click reads the needed public content.

## 0:20–0:50 — Evidence you can inspect

Choose **Try a fictional demo → Warning signals** and click **Check this store**.

“This merchant and these reports are fictional. The same response contract powers live scans.”

Show the heuristic risk index and separate evidence coverage. Expand a buyer report, its source link, and the allegation/verification labels. Explain that verified wording does not prove an allegation true.

## 0:50–1:10 — Financial confidence becomes action

Show the amount at stake, reasons to pause, and payment guidance. Explain that detected payment badges and general guidance are distinguished, and that eligibility/terms matter. Point to the next steps a shopper can actually take.

## 1:10–1:35 — Honest uncertainty and reliability

Switch to **Limited evidence**, then **Partial outage**.

“Missing sources do not turn into reassurance or an accusation. We show which checks were available and why others did not contribute.”

Open the coverage explanation. Note that a low index at low coverage does not establish trustworthiness. One failed provider does not erase the remaining results.

## 1:35–1:50 — Responsible AI

“AI helps research public discussions and, optionally, summarize the evidence. Review wording is compared locally, with no paid detector and no claim that repeated wording proves fraud. Code computes the final index, and the weak review-pattern source cannot create a high-risk result by itself.”

## 1:50–2:00 — Track fit and close

“For FNB, this turns information into a more understandable purchase decision and a practical next move. For Nittany AI, it applies AI to an everyday problem while making its limits visible. The shopper makes the final decision.”

Be ready to explain source verification, sparse evidence, provider outages, privacy boundaries, unvalidated scoring weights, and what still requires live evaluation.
